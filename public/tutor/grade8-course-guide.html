This original Grade 8 collection contains 18 chapters, 42 subchapters and 60 voice lessons. It brings together common number, algebra, geometry, measurement and data topics. It is not an exhaustive import of every country’s chapters, a textbook reproduction, or an official curriculum certification.

How a voice class works

The tutor introduces the selected skill, explains its meaning, works through two examples, asks whether you understand, and waits for your answer. Four guided questions follow. You then complete a ten-question timed exercise, receive a score and review mistakes. You can pause, resume, revisit another lesson or continue to the next topic.

Browser speech reads prepared teaching aloud without an AI request. A microphone is optional: type responses when it is unavailable. A supported browser, HTTPS and explicit permission are needed for spoken answers. Natural questions can use the existing live AI conversation when its connection is available. Real microphone and loudspeaker hardware were not verified by automated tests.

Results and checkpoints use the existing Netlify Database-backed progress service, within its browser-session and retention limits. They are not a permanent student account. Each Grade 8 attempt is stored separately so a full course does not become one oversized progress record. Grade 7 history remains separate.

Official material checked

  • Common Core State Standards Initiative: Grade 8 introduction and overview. Checked its number-system, expressions/equations, functions, geometry and bivariate-data scope. This is a domain reference, not a claim of covering every standard or adopting it for every US state.
  • England: mathematics programmes of study, Key Stage 3. Checked its number, algebra, ratio/proportion, geometry/measurement, probability and statistics content. This applies across Years 7–9. England does not prescribe the chapter order in this course as an official Year 8 sequence.
  • South Africa: Mathematics CAPS, Grades 7–9. Retrieved the official 164-page PDF and checked progression tables and Grade 8 teaching guidance, beginning at printed page 75. The course draws on the Senior Phase strands; shared topics may fall in another grade within that phase.

Not verified in this update: the NCERT Grade 8 PDF could not be retrieved. The NCERT portal remains a reference for families, but no edition-specific Indian chapter alignment is claimed. Australian, Canadian, Singaporean, Brazilian, Japanese and other national curricula were not verified or fully imported in this update.

What the course does not certify

Grade placement varies, especially for sets, compound growth, geometric sequences, inequalities and advanced solids. The final modelling/algorithm chapter is explicitly a stage-wide extension. Select lessons to match a student’s readiness and school sequence.

The ten-question score measures focused supporting skills, sometimes a single component such as a coefficient or coordinate. It does not assess every part of a chapter, all representations, open-ended proof, or independent mathematical modelling. Examples and practice use authored parameter variations, not adaptive AI-generated assessments.

The construction lesson includes real compass-and-straightedge work and a separate review rubric. A teacher or parent must inspect the construction; the numerical exercise cannot certify it. Automated tests check catalogue structure, calculations and simulated classroom interaction, not official accreditation or hardware audio.
