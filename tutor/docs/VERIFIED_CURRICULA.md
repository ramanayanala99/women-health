# Additional source-checked mathematics pathways

Checked 7 October 2026. Country selection is a directory filter, not a promise that every local syllabus is imported. Exact country, region, board/program and grade must match an available pack. Unsupported profiles are not substituted.

## England

United Kingdom → England → UK National Curriculum (England) → National Curriculum (England) → Years 7, 8 or 9.

Six official subject-content headings, represented by 65 app learning objectives. This is the full Key Stage 3 programme for Years 7–9, not an official year-by-year sequence. Each year selector opens that stage-wide programme at the selected teaching level; schools decide sequencing. `KS3.*` identifiers are app-owned and not ministry standard codes. Objectives are paraphrased, including grouped subskills. Chapter headings are programme strands rather than textbook chapters.

Official source:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-mathematics-programmes-of-study

## California

United States → California → Common Core → Common Core → Grade 8.

All 28 parent Grade 8 standard codes: 8.NS.1–2, 8.EE.1–8, 8.F.1–5, 8.G.1–9, 8.SP.1–4. Subparts are described within parent objectives. Five domain groups form chapters; these are standards domains rather than a textbook table of contents. Applies to California only, not an assumption about other states.

Official source: California Common Core State Standards: Mathematics, adopted 2010, modified 2013, PDF pages 60–63 (printed pages 54–56).
https://www.cde.ca.gov/be/st/ss/documents/ccssmathstandardaug2013.pdf

## Teaching coverage

Each objective has a stable central maths concept mapping. Existing concepts are reused; new concepts enter the central registry with no pretend prepared lesson. Source checking of an objective is separate from full lesson/assessment coverage. A prepared central lesson is a related introduction. A missing topic can be generated as an original English numeric-practice introduction, checked and shared through the updated backend. Neither automatically certifies full coverage of multi-part standards, proofs or open-ended requirements.

Original Ontario and CBSE pathways, voice choices, cloned voices, talking teacher photos, microphone conversation, diagrams, worksheets, progress and written chapter exams remain available. Chapter completion requires study records for all subtopics, and written exam submissions continue to await review.
